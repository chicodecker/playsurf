Privacy policy

PlaySurf has no accounts and asks for no personal data to play. This page explains the little data that is used, and why.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Who is responsible: Decker Games (Francisco Decker), Brazil · playsurfgame@gmail.com

In short: you can play PlaySurf without an account and without giving us any personal data. We count visits without cookies, your game progress stays on your device, we only see your name and e-mail if you write to us, and we never sell personal data. The game page shows ads from Google, which has its own privacy policy.

1. Visit statistics

We use GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly statistics service, to count visits and a few game events (for example, that a wave was played, or a range for how many times the game was opened on the same device). It records the page, the referring site, the browser, the screen size and the country. From the referring site and the page address we also count, as a category only, where a visit came from: one of our ads, Google, another search engine, a social network, another site, or a direct visit. GoatCounter sets no cookies and does not keep IP addresses, and the counts cannot tell us who you are.

2. Data saved on your device

The game saves your settings, choices, scores and progress in your browser's local storage, together with a simple count of how many times you have played on that device. This data stays on your device; we do not receive it, apart from the anonymous play-count range mentioned above. You can delete it at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings.

3. The contact form and e-mail

If you use the contact form, you send us your name, e-mail address, country and message. The form is delivered to our mailbox by Web3Forms. If you e-mail playsurfgame@gmail.com, we receive your message and address. We use them only to read and answer you, we keep them only as long as needed for that, and we delete them if you ask.

4. Ads from Google

To help keep PlaySurf free, the game page can load Google's ad service (Google AdSense for games), which may show an ad between menu screens. It is loaded only if you accept the cookie banner; if you decline, the ad service is not loaded. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show, limit and measure ads, and, where allowed, to personalise them. This is described in How Google uses information from sites that use its services and in Google's privacy policy. You can manage personalised ads from Google at My Ad Center.

4b. Ad and conversion cookies (Google Ads)

When you visit the game, a short banner asks whether you accept Google Ads cookies. If you accept, Google Ads may store cookies on your device to measure our advertising, for example whether a visit from an ad led to a first wave being played (we send Google only that a first wave was played, not who you are). If you decline, we do not allow these cookies, and Google receives only signals that do not use cookies or identify you. Your choice is kept in your browser's local storage, and you can change it by clearing this site's data.

The ad service in section 4 is loaded only after you accept this banner. If you decline, it is not loaded, and the game sets no ad cookies.

5. Services that deliver the site

Like any website, the services that deliver PlaySurf receive your IP address and basic browser information when your browser requests something from them. They are:

  • GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which hosts the website;
  • Google Fonts, for the PlaySurf font;
  • jsDelivr, which delivers the open-source 3D library used by the game;
  • Google AdSense, for the ads described above;
  • GoatCounter, for the statistics described above;
  • Web3Forms, only when you send the contact form.

Each of them handles that data under its own privacy policy. Links to other sites, such as Spotify, take you to services with their own policies.

6. Children

PlaySurf is a game for all ages and does not ask players for personal information. Children should ask a parent or guardian before using the contact form. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, write to us and we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, laws such as Brazil's LGPD and the European GDPR give you the right to ask what personal data we hold about you, and to have it corrected or deleted. In practice the only personal data we may hold is a message you sent us. Write to playsurfgame@gmail.com and we will answer.

8. Changes

If PlaySurf starts using data in a new way, we will update this page and the date at the top.
