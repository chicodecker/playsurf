The real wave

Þorlákshöfn (often written Thorlakshofn) is a small fishing town on the south coast of Iceland, less than an hour's drive from Reykjavík. Its wave breaks over rock next to the western wall of the harbour and is the best-known surf spot in the country: a powerful wave, best known as a right but breaking left as well, fed by the swells of the North Atlantic. The water is cold all year, so surfers paddle out in thick wetsuits with hood, gloves and boots. Local surfers have campaigned to protect the wave from plans to expand the harbour.

Surfing Þorlákshöfn in PlaySurf

In PlaySurf, Þorlákshöfn is the eighth wave and the coldest: a dark, heavy peak in near-black North Atlantic water, with snowy mountains along the shore, icebergs drifting out to sea and a low grey sky. Like the pool, Pipeline and Trestles, it breaks both ways, so you pick the left or the right before the drop. The ride lasts about 30 seconds: a barrel right off the drop, a ramp for an air, foam sections one after another, and a closeout that turns the whole wall white. Your surfer wears a full black wetsuit here, and nobody drops in on you: it is too cold for the local in boardshorts.

How to surf Þorlákshöfn

  1. The barrel comes right off the drop: pull in early and hold Shift to let it catch you.
  2. After the barrel, use the ramp: hit the lip fast with Space held to launch an air.
  3. The wave closes out white at the end, so land your best moves before the last section.

FAQ

Is Þorlákshöfn a left or a right?

Both in PlaySurf: it is a peak, and you pick the left or the right before the drop. In real life it is best known as a right.

How cold is the water at Þorlákshöfn?

In the game the water is 4 °C, so your surfer wears a full wetsuit. In real life Icelandic surfers wear thick wetsuits with hood, gloves and boots all year round.
