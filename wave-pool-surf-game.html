The real wave

Wave pools changed surfing: a machine pushes the same perfect wave down the pool again and again, so every ride starts in the same place and every turn can be repeated until it is right. That is why surfers use them to train, and why the first wave in PlaySurf is a pool.

Surfing PlaySurf Pool in PlaySurf

The PlaySurf Pool throws a long, clean wave that breaks to suit your stance, with a short barrel section, a wall for snaps and a closing section at the end. You can surf it in daylight or at night under the floodlights, with the neon sign and the crowd in the stands. It is the best place to learn the controls: drop in, set your bottom turn, hit the lip and look for the barrel when the lip starts to throw.

Tips

  • Your speed comes from the bottom turn: go low before you go high.
  • When the lip starts to pitch, stay high and hold Shift to wait for the barrel.
  • Hit the lip fast with Space held to launch an air.

How to unlock it

The pool is open from the start. A score of 5 here opens Uluwatu.

FAQ

Can I surf the wave pool at night?

Yes. Pick Night in the wave menu and the pool is lit by floodlights.

Is the pool a left or a right?

It breaks to suit your stance, so on your first rides you always surf facing the wave.
