The real wave

The Pororoca is a tidal bore: when a strong tide meets the current at the mouth of the Amazon and its tributaries, a wave forms and travels upriver for kilometres. Its name comes from an indigenous Tupi word for a great roar. It is strongest around the new and full moons near the equinoxes, and surfers ride it for many minutes on a single wave.

Surfing Pororoca in PlaySurf

In PlaySurf, the Pororoca is the longest ride in the game: a thick wall of brown water and heavy white water running up a river between two walls of rainforest, with no barrel and no end in sight. Ride the face, stay ahead of the foam and float across the sections. It is open from the start.

Tips

  • Stay ahead of the white water: it keeps coming behind you.
  • Hit the breaking lip and the floater takes you along the top.
  • Pace yourself: this ride is much longer than the others.
