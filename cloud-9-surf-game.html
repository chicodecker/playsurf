The real wave

Cloud 9 is the wave that put Siargao Island, in the Philippines, on the surfing map: a hollow right-hand reef break near General Luna, famous for its thick barrels and the wooden boardwalk that runs out over the reef, where people watch the surfers from just a few metres away.

Surfing Cloud 9 in PlaySurf

In PlaySurf, Cloud 9 is a shorter, punchier right with a barrel that throws fast. The trick is timing: set a clean bottom turn, climb, and when the lip starts to pitch, brake to let the barrel close over you. Come out of it with speed and you still have a wall for one or two turns.

Tips

  • Brake (Shift) a moment before the lip throws, not after.
  • Stay on the high line inside the barrel to come out clean.
  • On the shoulder, a snap scores well after a barrel.

How to unlock it

Score 6 at Uluwatu to open Cloud 9. A 6.5 here opens Pipeline.

FAQ

Is Cloud 9 a left or a right?

A right-hander. Regular-footers surf it facing the wave.

Is the boardwalk in the game?

The wave is the star here: a fast right-hand barrel over the reef.
