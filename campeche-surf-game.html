The real wave

Campeche is one of the best-known beaches of Florianópolis, on Santa Catarina Island in the south of Brazil, with Campeche Island just offshore. Its sandbanks line up long right-handers on the right swell, and the lineup is always busy with locals.

Surfing Campeche in PlaySurf

In PlaySurf, Campeche is a long right-hander with the island behind it and a lineup full of surfers paddling back to the peak. It rewards flow: long carves, snaps and cutbacks linked down a wall that keeps going. Watch out for the drop-in local who tries to steal your wave.

Tips

  • Keep your speed for the long walls: link turns without stopping.
  • The drop-in local is part of the show: deal with him and keep surfing.
  • Cutbacks keep you close to the power on the long shoulder.
