PlaySurf is a free 3D surfing game that runs right in your web browser, on a computer or a phone, with no download and no account. Paddle into the wave, pop up and ride it the way a surfer does: speed comes from your bottom turn, the lip pitches over your head in the barrel, and judges score every ride.

There are eighteen waves to surf, from a perfect wave pool to the heaving barrels of Pipeline, the long left walls of Uluwatu, the A-frame peak at Lower Trestles, the endless Amazon river bore, the Pororoca, and a cold arctic peak at Þorlákshöfn, in Iceland. Five more take you around the world: the heavy beach-break barrels of Supertubos, in Portugal, the endless left barrel of Skeleton Bay, in Namibia, the Greenbush slab in the Mentawai Islands, the Supertubes right at Jeffreys Bay, in South Africa, and Chicama, in Peru, the longest left in the world. Four more are new: the wedging left of Matadeiro and the left of Rosa Norte, both in Santa Catarina, Brazil, the perfect left of Restaurants, in Fiji, and the giant barrels of Ilha dos Lobos, off Torres, the biggest wave in the game. And the newest is Safi, in Morocco: Le Jardin, a long, heavy right-hand point under ochre cliffs, one barrel after another. Rain, sun showers, dawn and night sessions change every ride.

Made by a surfer

PlaySurf is an indie game. Its creator, a surfer, waited decades for a surf game that felt like the real thing, and when none came, made one: to play with a son and a wife who surf too, in Party Surf sessions at home. Knowing surfing from the water, every move was tuned again and again until the riding felt fluid and had style. The idea is to offer it free to anyone who wants to have fun, so that even people who live far from the beach can feel what surfing is like.

How to play

Computer: arrow keys steer on the wave face, Space crouches and launches an air at the lip, Shift brakes for the barrel, Option/Alt does a cutback, G a carve 360, C changes the camera and P pauses. Gamepads work too.

Phone and tablet: steer with your thumb and use the on-screen buttons for air, carve, cutback, layback and brake. Turn the phone sideways for the best view.

FAQ

Is PlaySurf free?

Yes. PlaySurf is completely free and runs in your browser with no download or sign-up.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open playsurfgame.com in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox, on a computer or a phone.

How do I unlock the other waves?

All 19 waves are open from the start: pick any of them on the map in the wave menu.

How do I get barreled?

Stay high on the face as the lip starts to throw, then hold Shift to brake and let the barrel catch up with you.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, with touch controls made for phones and tablets.
