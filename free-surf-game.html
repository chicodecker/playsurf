PlaySurf is a free 3D surfing game that runs right in your web browser, with no download and no account. Paddle into a wave pool and ride a perfect left-hander, in clear blue water by day or under the floodlights at night. Judges score every wave, and at the end you get a replay and photos of your session.

Made by Decker Games for people who actually surf: speed comes from your bottom turn, just like in the ocean, and every maneuver reads the wave.

Tricks

Bottom turn
drop in and aim at the base of the wave to build speed.
Snap
climb and tap at the top to whip the tail and come back down.
Re-entry
go straight up to the lip and let the board pivot.
Roundhouse cutback
on the shoulder, swing back to the whitewater and rebound to the face.
Barrel
stay high on the face as the lip throws and brake to get deeper.
Floater
ride over the breaking section on top of the foam.
Aerial
hit the lip fast, launch, spin, flip and grab.

How to play

On a computer, use the arrow keys to point where you want to go on the wave face, Space to crouch and launch off the lip, and Shift to stall for the barrel. Enter starts paddling, N switches day and night, and C changes the camera. The in-game text is currently in Portuguese.

FAQ

Is PlaySurf free?

Yes. PlaySurf is completely free and runs in your browser with no download or sign-up.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Just open playsurfgame.com in a modern browser such as Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox.

What tricks can I do?

Bottom turns, snaps, tail slides, laybacks, re-entries, roundhouse cutbacks, barrels, floaters and aerials with spins, flips and grabs.

Can I surf at night?

Yes. Press N or pick Night in the menu to surf the pool under the floodlights.
