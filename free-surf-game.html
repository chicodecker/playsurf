PlaySurf is a free 3D surfing game that runs right in your web browser, on a computer or a phone, with no download and no account. There are seven waves to surf, from a perfect wave pool to the heaving barrels of Pipeline, the long left walls of Uluwatu, the A-frame peak at Lower Trestles and the endless Amazon river bore, the Pororoca. Rain, sun showers, dawn and night sessions change every ride.

Seven waves to surf

  • PlaySurf Pool – a perfect wave in a wave pool, by day or under the floodlights. The place to learn.
  • Uluwatu – long, fast left walls off the cliffs of Bali.
  • Cloud 9 – the famous right-hand barrel of Siargao, in the Philippines.
  • Pipeline – the heaviest barrel in the world, with Backdoor going right.
  • Lower Trestles – a California A-frame with lefts and rights for snaps and airs.
  • Campeche – a long right-hander in Florianópolis, Brazil.
  • Pororoca – the Amazon tidal bore: one wave that runs for kilometres up a river.

How to play

Computer: arrow keys steer on the wave face, Space crouches and launches an air at the lip, Shift brakes for the barrel, Option/Alt does a cutback, G a carve 360, C changes the camera and P pauses. Gamepads work too.

Phone and tablet: steer with your thumb and use the on-screen buttons for air, carve, cutback, layback and brake. Turn the phone sideways for the best view.

FAQ

Is PlaySurf free?

Yes. PlaySurf is completely free and runs in your browser with no download or sign-up.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open playsurfgame.com in Chrome, Safari, Edge or Firefox, on a computer or a phone.

How do I unlock the other waves?

Score well on one wave and the next opens: the pool leads to Uluwatu, then Cloud 9, Pipeline, Lower Trestles and Campeche. The Pororoca is open from the start.

How do I get barreled?

Stay high on the face as the lip starts to throw, then hold Shift to brake and let the barrel catch up with you.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes, with touch controls made for phones and tablets.
